About

I am an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at the University of Birmingham. Before that I read Computer Science at Ningbo University, and spent two years on compulsory military service in the PLA Navy.

My work so far has been data-centric: deciding which training examples are worth keeping, before any model is trained on them. My most recent project, RMD2, fits a single class-conditional geometry on frozen embeddings and reuses it for three jobs — ranking noisy candidates, constraining a continuous generator, and building preference pairs for a language model — without training a task-specific proxy for any of them.

Publications

RMD2: One Geometry, Two Roles in Data Curation and Generation Guidance

2026

Co-first author Under review — AAAI 2027

A class-conditional conformity geometry that needs no task-proxy training. For each class we fit an MRCD robust covariance and a regularised precision matrix on a small trusted reference set in a frozen embedding space; an inter-class Mahalanobis margin — rather than an absolute distance — decides whether a sample agrees with the label it was given. The fitted geometry then exposes three interfaces: the margin ranks candidates for curation and for discrete preference pairs, while the analytic gradient of the within-class distance constrains continuous generators.

  • 98.8 / 99.9%detection AUROC on CIFAR-10N / CLINC150
  • 0.56 / 0.02%residual noise left in the selected set
  • 19.7×less peak memory than the runner-up; fastest of eight methods
  • −11.0%FID when the same geometry guides a conditional GAN
