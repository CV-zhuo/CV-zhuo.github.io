About

I am an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at the University of Birmingham. Before that I read Computer Science at Ningbo University.

My work so far has been data-centric: deciding which training examples are worth keeping before any model is trained on them, and doing it cheaply enough to re-run every time the data pool changes. My most recent project, RMD2, scores how well a sample fits the class it was labelled with — using only a small trusted reference set and a frozen encoder — and reuses that one signal for three jobs: ranking noisy candidates, constraining a continuous generator, and building preference pairs for a language model, without training a task-specific proxy for any of them.

I am also interested in continual learning: how a model keeps taking in new knowledge without overwriting what it already has, and how the choice of training data decides what survives. To me it is the same question as curation seen from the other side — curation asks what is worth learning, continual learning asks what is worth keeping.

I am looking for a PhD position starting in 2027. If this overlaps with your group's work, I would be glad to hear from you: zhuoshihao99@gmail.com. A full CV is here.

Publications and Patents

RMD2: One Geometry, Two Roles in Data Curation and Generation Guidance

2026

Co-first author In preparation — PVLDB

A low-cost curation operator for large data pipelines. Class statistics are fitted once from a small trusted reference set in a frozen embedding space; after that, scoring a candidate is one encoder pass and one quadratic form, with no task-proxy model trained anywhere in the loop. The contribution is to judge label consistency by an inter-class margin rather than an absolute distance, and to make the same fitted statistics serve both noisy-sample curation and generation guidance.

Multi-Center MRI Image Classification Method

2024.08

Invention patent, granted CN117173489B First inventor

A vertical loss separates the content features of an image from its style features, so that the classifier attends only to content. This mitigates heterogeneity between imaging centers and strengthens cross-center generalisation.

A Sequential Multi-Classification Method for sMRI Images

2024.08

Invention patent, granted CN116740463B Fifth inventor

A ranking model keeps distances between class feature vectors consistent with their distances along the actual pathological progression. Rather than treating the classes as mutually independent labels, the design carries the ordinal structure of disease progression into the feature space, improving multi-class performance.

sMRI Classification with a High-Resolution Complementary-Attention UNet

2022.07

Invention patent, granted CN114332535B Fifth inventor

Feature-fusion and classification branches inserted into UNet supply semantic information to the encoder and detail to the decoder. The classification branch fuses multi-semantic feature maps to raise the network's representational capacity and, with it, classification accuracy.
